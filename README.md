## How it works

`main.ts` writes the forecast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), UTF-8, no trailing newline, mode 0644.

## Entry text

> Snoozington: heavy blankets of fog until 9, clearing to scattered naps by noon, with a 90% chance of your train dreaming through your stop.

139 characters, one line. Built for the judge brief: an invented city is named, the register stays broadcast-forecast (fog until 9, clearing, a percentage chance), and one concrete detail — the dreaming train — makes the place feel real.
